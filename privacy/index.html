4. Why we use your personal data and the legal basis

We process personal data on the following legal bases:

To provide our services (contract performance)

Creating and managing accounts, delivering features you use, processing payments, and providing support.

To communicate with you (legitimate interests / contract)

Responding to enquiries, sending service updates, and notifying you of changes to our services.

To improve our services (legitimate interests / consent for analytics)

Analysing how our website and software are used so we can fix problems and build better features.

To comply with legal obligations (legal obligation)

Keeping records required by tax, accounting, or other applicable laws.

To send marketing messages (consent or legitimate interests)

We will only send marketing emails where you have opted in, or where you are an existing business contact and you can opt out at any time. Every marketing email contains an unsubscribe link.

7. Your rights

Under applicable data protection law, you have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data in certain circumstances
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Objection — object to our processing based on legitimate interests
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time
  • Complaint — lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email admin@unifynow.digital. We will respond within one month.

If you are in the UK, you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). If you are in the EU, you may complain to your national data protection authority.
